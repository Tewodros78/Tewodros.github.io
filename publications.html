Predictability of Foreign Aid and Economic Growth in Ethiopia

Gutema, T. G. &Tilahun, S.(2022). Cogent Economics & Finance , 11, Article 2098606. https://doi.org/10.1080/23322039.2022.2098606.

This study examined the contribution of foreign aid and the macroeconomic policy environment to economic growth in the country. In order to meet the aforementioned objective, the study employed an autoregressive distributed lag (ARDL) approach over the period 1985–2019. The empirical findings show that foreign aid has a positive role in economic growth in the long run, but its short-run effect is insignificant.
